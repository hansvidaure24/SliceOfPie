OCR benchmark

Renders each fixture in test/fixtures/receipts/ as clean, photo-like, faded and small synthetic images, then runs every engine on them. Character error rate (CER) is measured against the fixture text. "Parse exact" means the parser's output from the OCR text matches its output from the true text; "Amounts exact" only requires every price and the tax to match. Timings are from this machine, not a phone.
